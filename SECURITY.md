# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Do not** report security issues via a public issue, pull request, or discussion. Please use one of the following channels:

- **GitHub:** Click on the **Security** tab in this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
- **Email:** Send a message to <dv_het.oprachtenteam@amsterdam.nl>.

When reporting, please include (if possible):

- Which part is affected (for example, api-server, auth-server, admin-server, cms-server, image-server, or a widget);
- The version or commit;
- Steps to reproduce the issue;
- The potential impact.

## What to Expect from Us

- We will acknowledge your report within **3 working days**.
- Within **10 working days**, you will receive an initial assessment and an expected timeline for resolution.
- We will keep you informed until the issue is resolved and will credit you when publishing the solution, unless you prefer otherwise.

Please do not publish details about the vulnerability until a fix is available, or unless agreed upon with us.
